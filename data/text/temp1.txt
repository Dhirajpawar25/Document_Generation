01 Affidavit Format Explained
Affidavit in Reply — Bombay High Court, writ matter, short reply
Dated: 05-09-2026
An Affidavit in Reply is a sworn statement filed by a respondent to answer and oppose a petition. It denies the petitioner's case, states the respondent's
version, and asks the Court to dismiss the petition. It follows a fixed skeleton, and almost every sentence uses a set phrase.
1. The ten parts
All ten appear, in this order. None are optional.
# Part Form Rules
1 Forum heading IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT [CITY] First line. Bold, caps, centred.
2 Jurisdiction [TYPE] JURISDICTION Bold, caps, centred. Always ends with the word JURISDICTION. E.g.
Civil Appellate; Ordinary Original Civil.
3 Case number [PROCEEDING] NO. [NUMBER] OF [YEAR] Bold, caps, centred. NO. and OF are fixed words.
4 Cause title Names left, status tags right Tags begin with three dots: ...Petitioner, ...Respondent
No.1. VERSUS on its own centred line. Respondents numbered 1.,
2., 3.
5 Affidavit title AFFIDAVIT IN REPLY ON BEHALF OF RESPONDENT NO. Bold, caps, centred. Always carries the respondent number.
[N]
6 Deponent clause I, [NAME], ... do hereby solemnly affirm and One sentence, not numbered. See the deponent rule below.
state as under:
7 Numbered paragraphs 1. 2. 3. ... Number bold, then full stop. One continuous sequence. Prayer is
not part of it.
8 Prayer (a) (b) (c) Heading PRAYER bold caps. Letters bold. Lettered, never
numbered with the body.
9 Jurat Solemnly affirmed at [PLACE] / On this [Nth] day DEPONENT right, caps. Before Me left. Verb must match Part 6.
of [Month] [Year]
10 Verification ... contents of paragraphs 1 to [N] and the Heading bold caps. Range must match the actual body count.
Prayer ... Place and date repeat the jurat.
2. Deponent rule (Part 6)
Respondent is a person → that person deposes: “the Respondent No.2 above named”.
Respondent is a company or authority → an officer deposes for it: “the [designation] of the Respondent No.2 above named”. Never write “I am the
Respondent No.2” for an organisation.
Verb agreement: solemnly affirm → Solemnly affirmed; swear and affirm → Sworn.
3. Paragraph sequence (Part 7)
Para Move Opening words
1 Identity and perusal “I say that I am the Respondent No.__ in the above Writ Petition and am well acquainted with the facts and
circumstances of the case...”
2 Blanket denial “At the outset, I deny each and every allegation, contention and submission made in the Writ Petition, save and
except those specifically admitted herein...”
3 Preliminary position “I say that the Petitioner has suppressed material facts... The action complained of has been taken strictly in
accordance with law...”
4 onward Substantive answers “With reference to the averments made in the Petition, I say that the same are false, incorrect and denied...” — one
paragraph per point answered
Last Closing “In the premises aforesaid, I say that the Writ Petition deserves to be dismissed with costs.”
4. Fixed phrases
Used in Phrase
Deponent clause the Respondent No.__ above named · do hereby solemnly affirm and state as under:
Paragraph 1 am well acquainted with the facts and circumstances of the case · I have perused the Petition and the documents
annexed thereto · am competent to affirm this Affidavit in Reply
Blanket denial At the outset, I deny each and every allegation, contention and submission · save and except those specifically admitted
herein · misconceived, devoid of merits and is liable to be dismissed in limine
Preliminary position has suppressed material facts · strictly in accordance with law and after following due procedure · No legal, constitutional
or fundamental right... has been infringed
Substantive answer With reference to the averments made in the Petition · the same are false, incorrect and denied · has failed to make out
any case warranting interference · the extraordinary writ jurisdiction of this Hon'ble Court
Closing In the premises aforesaid · deserves to be dismissed with costs
Prayer I therefore respectfully pray that this Hon'ble Court may be pleased to: · grant such other and further reliefs as this
Hon'ble Court may deem fit and proper
Verification true and correct to my knowledge and belief · nothing material has been concealed therefrom
Date format: ordinal day + month + year — 5th day of September 2026.
5. Formatting
Element Format
Forum heading, jurisdiction, case number, affidavit title Bold, ALL CAPS, centred
PRAYER, VERIFICATION headings Bold, ALL CAPS, centred
Cause title party names Normal, left
Status tags (...Petitioner) Right-aligned
