# Sample Reference Document

This is a compact, safe reference sample for testing the application. It demonstrates the structural contract expected by `parse_reference_document`. Replace party names and facts only in the case-information file; keep the structure and fixed phrases stable.

```text
IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT BOMBAY
CIVIL APPELLATE JURISDICTION
WRIT PETITION NO. 3147 OF 2026
Arjun Mehta, Age 38 years,
Occupation: Business,
residing at Mumbai. ...Petitioner
VERSUS
1. State of Maharashtra,
Through the Principal Secretary,
Mumbai. ...Respondent No.1
2. Rohan Deshpande, Age 42 years,
residing at Mumbai. ...Respondent No.2
AFFIDAVIT IN REPLY ON BEHALF OF RESPONDENT NO. 2
I, Rohan Deshpande, Age 42 years, residing at Mumbai, the Respondent No.2 above named,
do hereby solemnly affirm and state as under:
1. I say that I am the Respondent No.2 in the above Writ Petition and am well acquainted
with the facts and circumstances of the case. I have perused the Petition and the documents
annexed thereto and am competent to affirm this Affidavit in Reply.
2. At the outset, I deny each and every allegation, contention and submission made in the
Writ Petition, save and except those specifically admitted herein.
3. I say that the action complained of has been taken strictly in accordance with law and
after following due procedure.
4. With reference to the averments made in the Petition, I say that the same are false,
incorrect and denied. Hereto annexed and marked as EXHIBIT-‘A’ is a copy of the letter.
5. In the premises aforesaid, I say that the Writ Petition deserves to be dismissed with costs.
PRAYER
I therefore respectfully pray that this Hon’ble Court may be pleased to:
(a) dismiss the present Writ Petition with costs;
(b) refuse any interim or ad-interim relief sought by the Petitioner; and
(c) grant such other and further reliefs as this Hon’ble Court may deem fit and proper.
Solemnly affirmed at Mumbai
On this 5th day of September 2026
Before Me DEPONENT
VERIFICATION
I, Rohan Deshpande, the Deponent above named, do hereby verify that the contents of
paragraphs 1 to 5 and the Prayer above are true and correct to my knowledge and belief.
Verified at Mumbai on this 5th day of September 2026.
DEPONENT
MEHTA & KULKARNI
Advocates for the Respondent No.2.
```

## Required reference conventions

- Include the court, jurisdiction, proceeding number, cause title, and respondent labels.
- Include a deponent clause containing “do hereby solemnly affirm and state as under”.
- Include continuous numbered body paragraphs.
- Include `PRAYER`, lettered prayer items `(a)`, `(b)`, and `(c)`.
- Include the jurat, `VERIFICATION`, `DEPONENT`, and advocate block.
- Use `EXHIBIT-` for the exhibit convention.
